One ecosystem for campus life.

UniVerse is designed to bring the fragmented student experience into one connected digital environment. Students shouldn't need to jump between countless disconnected platforms to communicate, study and participate in campus life. UniVerse brings those experiences together.

The ecosystem

Four systems. One campus.

System 01

Orbit

Your campus social layer. Connect with people around you, discover what's happening and participate in campus life.

System 02

Study Hub

Your academic command center. Access learning resources, past questions, study materials and tools designed to make studying easier.

System 03

Campus Chat

Talk to your campus. Communicate directly with other students inside your campus ecosystem.

System 04

Study Tribes

Learn together, go further. Create or join focused learning communities and study with people pursuing the same goals.
